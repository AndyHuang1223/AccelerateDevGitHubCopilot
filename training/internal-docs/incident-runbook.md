# Incident Runbook

1. Confirm the incident scope and preserve the correlation ID.
2. Check health and dependency status before changing application state.
3. Prefer read-only investigation and capture timestamps, request IDs, and evidence.
4. Escalate authentication, authorization, data-loss, or production-write incidents to the service owner.
5. Do not use an agent to merge, deploy, delete infrastructure, or modify production data without explicit approval.
